RoomFit: Pack Every Inch — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026
App: RoomFit: Pack Every Inch (Android package com.ninjanext.roomfit)
Developer: NinjaNext Technologies — http://ninjanexttech.com/
Contact: info@ninjanexttech.com

RoomFit is a fully offline packing puzzle game. It needs no account, has no servers, and works without an internet connection. This policy explains what the game stores and confirms that we don't collect it.

Information the game stores on your device

To save your progress, RoomFit stores the following only on your device, in the app's local storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Level progress: completed levels, star ratings and best move counts
  • Daily puzzle completions and streaks
  • Achievements and hint credits
  • Settings such as sound and vibration

Information we collect

We don't collect, receive, sell or share any personal information. RoomFit has no user accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The game doesn't send any information over the internet. NinjaNext can't see your progress.

Network use

The only time the game opens a network connection is when you voluntarily tap an outbound link — for example a Google Play listing of another NinjaNext app, or this privacy policy. Those links open in your browser or the Play Store app, which are governed by their own privacy policies. The game itself sends nothing.

Permissions

The game doesn't access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Reset your progress in the game's Settings
  • Clear the app's storage in your device settings
  • Uninstall the game, which removes its stored data

Because your progress exists only on your device, deleting it is permanent unless your device's own backup includes it. We can't recover it for you.

Children

RoomFit is intended for general audiences. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Future updates to the game may add optional features, such as advertising (for example Google AdMob). The game contains no advertising SDKs today. If a future version adds any feature that changes how data is handled, we'll update this policy and its effective date first, before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@ninjanexttech.com · http://ninjanexttech.com/
